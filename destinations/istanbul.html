About Istanbul

Istanbul is a captivating city known for its
historic mosques, bustling bazaars, Bosphorus views,
and unique blend of European and Asian cultures.

Travel Tips

· Dress respectfully when visiting mosques and religious sites.

· Use Istanbulkart for convenient public transport.

· Wear comfortable shoes for Istanbul's steep and uneven streets.

· Keep some Turkish Lira for small shops and local markets.

· Take a ferry across the Bosphorus for a unique city experience.
