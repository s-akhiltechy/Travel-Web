About Bangkok

Bangkok is a lively city known for golden temples,
bustling markets, flavorful street food,
and a vibrant mix of tradition and modern life.

Quick Information

Best Time :

November - February

Currency :

Thai Baht (฿)

Language :

Thai

Ideal Duration :

3 - 5 days

Top Places

Grand Palace

Bangkok's iconic royal
complex filled with
stunning architecture.

Wat Arun

A beautiful riverside
temple known for its
striking design.

Chatuchak Market

A huge weekend market
packed with food,
fashion, and crafts.

Khao San Road

A lively street famous
for nightlife, food, and
backpacker culture.

Chao Phraya River

A scenic river
offering cruises and
beautiful city views.

Travel Tips

· Dress modestly when visiting temples and royal sites.

· Use the BTS Skytrain and MRT to avoid heavy traffic.

· Carry small amounts of Thai Baht for markets and street vendors.

· Try street food at busy stalls with high local turnover.

· Carry an umbrella during the rainy season.
