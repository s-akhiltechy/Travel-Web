About Santorini

Santorini is a stunning Greek island known for its
whitewashed villages, volcanic landscapes, blue-domed churches,
and spectacular Aegean Sea views.

Travel Tips

· Book Oia sunset spots early during peak season.

· Wear comfortable shoes for Santorini's steep, uneven paths.

· Carry water, especially when exploring villages on foot.

· Use local buses or taxis for getting between major towns.

· Visit popular viewpoints early in the day for fewer crowds.
