About Da Nang

Da Nang is a vibrant coastal city known for
beautiful beaches, mountain landscapes, local food, and
easy access to some of Central Vietnam's most iconic sights.

Travel Tips

· Rent a scooter or use ride-hailing apps to get around easily.

· Visit Ba Na Hills early to avoid large crowds.

· Carry light clothing and stay hydrated in the tropical heat.

· Try local specialties like Mi Quang and Banh Xeo.

· Visit Dragon Bridge in the evening for its illuminated city views.
