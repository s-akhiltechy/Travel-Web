About Sydney

Sydney is a vibrant coastal city known for its
famous harbour, beautiful beaches, iconic landmarks,
and relaxed Australian lifestyle.

Travel Tips

· Use an Opal card or contactless payment for public transport.

· Swim between the flags at beaches such as Bondi for safer swimming.

· Apply sunscreen regularly, especially during outdoor activities.

· Start coastal walks early to avoid the strongest midday sun.

· Bring comfortable shoes for exploring Sydney's harbour and beaches.
