Travel Tips

· Get an Oyster card or use contactless payment for public transport.

· Carry a light rain jacket—the weather can change quickly.

· Book popular attractions ahead during busy travel seasons.

· Walk along the South Bank for great views of London's landmarks.

· Remember that cars drive on the left side of the road.
