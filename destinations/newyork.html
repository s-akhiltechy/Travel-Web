Travel Tips

· Use the subway for fast and affordable travel around Manhattan.

· Wear comfortable shoes—New York involves plenty of walking.

· Keep your belongings secure in crowded areas and on public transport.

· Consider visiting major attractions on weekdays to avoid the biggest crowds.
