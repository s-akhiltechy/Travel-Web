Travel Tips

· Visit outdoor attractions during the cooler morning or evening hours.

· Dress respectfully, especially when visiting religious or cultural sites.

· Use the Metro for convenient travel between major areas.

· Keep sunscreen, sunglasses, and water with you when outdoors.

· Book desert safaris and popular attractions in advance.
