About Amsterdam

Amsterdam is a charming city known for its
historic canals, cycling culture, beautiful museums,
narrow houses, and lively streets.

Travel Tips

· Watch carefully for bicycles when crossing Amsterdam's cycle lanes.

· Book the Anne Frank House well in advance.

· Use trams and metro for convenient city travel.

· Consider renting a bicycle only if you're confident cycling in busy traffic.

· Bring a light rain jacket as weather can change quickly.
