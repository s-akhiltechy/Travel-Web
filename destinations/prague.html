About Prague

Prague is a beautiful European city known for its
medieval architecture, historic squares, charming bridges,
and rich Czech culture.

Travel Tips

· Wear comfortable shoes for Prague's cobblestone streets.

· Use trams and the Metro for convenient city travel.

· Visit Charles Bridge early for a quieter experience.

· Carry Czech Koruna for smaller shops and local cafés.

· Watch your belongings in crowded tourist areas.
