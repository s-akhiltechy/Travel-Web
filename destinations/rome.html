Travel Tips

· Wear comfortable shoes—Rome is best explored on foot.

· Visit major attractions early morning to avoid peak crowds.

· Carry a reusable water bottle—Rome has many public drinking fountains.

· Dress appropriately when visiting churches and Vatican sites.

· Use the Metro and buses for longer journeys across the city.
