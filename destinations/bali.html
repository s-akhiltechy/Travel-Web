About Bali

Bali is a tropical paradise known for its
beautiful beaches, lush rice terraces,
ancient temples, and vibrant island culture.

Travel Tips

· Wear a sarong or appropriate clothing when visiting temples.

· Rent a scooter only if you're comfortable riding in Bali's traffic.

· Carry cash in Indonesian Rupiah for small shops and local markets.

· Start sightseeing early in the morning to avoid crowds and strong afternoon heat.

· Be respectful during Balinese religious ceremonies and offerings.
