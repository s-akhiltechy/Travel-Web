Travel Tips

· Get a Suica or PASMO card for convenient travel on trains and buses.

· Avoid rush hours if possible, especially on Tokyo's busiest train lines.

· Keep your voice low and follow quiet etiquette on public transport.

· Many small restaurants and shops may prefer cash, so carry some yen.

· Download a translation app for menus and signs outside major tourist areas.
