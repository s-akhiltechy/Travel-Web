About Shanghai

Shanghai is a dynamic city known for its
futuristic skyline, historic neighborhoods,
vibrant markets, and rich Chinese culture.

Quick Information

March - May

Currency :

Chinese Yuan (¥)

Language :

Ideal Duration :

Travel Tips

· Carry a translation app as English may be limited outside tourist areas.

· Use the Metro for fast and affordable travel across the city.

· Keep your passport with you when required for identification.

· Carry some Chinese Yuan for smaller shops and local markets.

· Visit the Bund after sunset for the best skyline views.
