About Barcelona

Barcelona is a vibrant coastal city known for
Gaudí's unique architecture, beautiful beaches,
rich culture, and unforgettable food.

· Keep your belongings secure in crowded tourist areas.

· Use the Metro for quick and affordable city travel.

· Book Sagrada Família tickets ahead of time.

· Try local tapas and traditional Catalan dishes.

· Wear comfortable shoes for exploring the city's streets.
